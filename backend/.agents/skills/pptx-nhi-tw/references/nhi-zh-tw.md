# NHI and zh-TW policy guidance

- Write all slide-visible text, chart labels, captions, and notes in 繁體中文 (`zh-TW`) unless a bilingual format is requested. Reuse the source’s official terminology exactly: 全民健康保險、健保署／中央健康保險署、衛生福利部、部分負擔、給付、特約醫事機構、點值、二代健保、補充保險費.
- Use a formal 工作報告／政策簡報 register. Do not invent, round, or extrapolate policy figures; every factual claim needs an EvidenceMap source.
- Follow the source’s date convention. If unspecified, use ROC plus Gregorian year, e.g. `113年（2024）`; never silently convert one convention to the other.
- Honor the job's detected CJK-safe font; prefer `Microsoft JhengHei` (微軟正黑體) when installed, with `Noto Sans CJK TC` as a suitable installed alternative. Use the reference-driven size hierarchy in `reference-style.md`: on a wide slide, generally 30–36pt content titles, 18–22pt body, 14–16pt chart/diagram labels, and 10–12pt sources. `DFKai-SB` (標楷體) is optional for a specifically requested formal cover/divider, not the default reference style. Keep roughly 1.2–1.4× Chinese body leading.
- Leave about 10% width slack in mixed CJK/Latin/numeric text. Sandbox rendering substitutes CJK fonts, so visually inspect output on a target Office environment when possible.
- Use a light visual system throughout the deck: white/off-white/light-gray backgrounds, dark navy or charcoal text, and restrained NHI blue/green accents. Keep contrast accessible and do not mix light and dark themes.
- The first slide must contain one official, unmodified logo from `assets/nhi_logo_large.png` or `assets/nhi_logo_small.png`. Preserve its aspect ratio, transparency, colors, and surrounding clear space. The wide logo is preferred when the cover grid allows it; the seal is the compact fallback.
